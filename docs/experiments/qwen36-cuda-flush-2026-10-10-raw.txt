--- reading ---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The prediction in #80 was, in substance, at most about the take wait
    (0.84 in the 9 October run) minus the query's own cost, which would
    land in issue. The 0.84 is another day's; in this session the OFF
    arm's wait was 0.51 (a per-arm mean, noise not estimated). The
    point estimate of the gain (-0.60) exceeds it, though the interval
    [-1.09, -0.11] does not exclude a smaller gain.
    Issue came out SMALLER (-0.29) and the wait slightly LARGER (0.51 ->
    0.59),
    and the rest of the gain in the DeltaNet, lm_head and attention
    timers, which contain no expert group. These are differences of
    per-arm means over six runs each, not paired, and their noise is
    not estimated; the paired figure is step() alone.
  - So the ~22 us between the call's return and the kernel is in the
    (profiled) trace, and the flush is worth -0.60 ms/token on this prompt, but this
    run does not show that the flush removed that wait: a trace with the
    flush on would. Everything the flush could touch outside the group
    (the next dense call on the default stream, the next launch) shares
    the same driver, and nothing here separates those.
  - Same hit rate, swaps, misses and residency in both arms: the flush
    moves no expert.
