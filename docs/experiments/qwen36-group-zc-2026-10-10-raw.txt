Reading: the variant is a gain on this prompt. The traces (one each, not
paired) show a shorter launch call and a longer down kernel; from call
start to results they differ by about 18 us x 40 layers = 0.7 ms/token
(profiled). With QT_ASYNC_ISSUE=1 the launch runs on the helper, so that
figure reaches the decode thread only through wait, and per layer through
max(C, h + L + P): it bounds what the group change can save, it is not an
amount that shows in the step. In the A/B's per-arm means (not paired) the
moe time falls 7.83 -> 6.67: wait by 0.50, about a third of the 1.38 step
gain, and shared by 0.55; another ~0.2 lies in attn, dn and head (2.12 ->
2.06, 4.17 -> 4.05, 1.06 -> 1.04). Neither the shared nor that part is
explained by the traces. Also not explained by write volume: in moe3 the
522 seven-expert groups have a longer down kernel than the 4466
eight-expert ones (median 44.6 against 38.1 us). COLI_CUDA_GROUP_ZC_OUT
(the change this record ships with) tests the down kernel's half: the down
writes VRAM again and a copy kernel writes the 64 KiB to host_y in 16-byte
stores by consecutive threads. With the variant on, QT_ASYNC_ISSUE still
buys nothing measurable; the synchronous launch now costs the decode thread
0.82 ms/token (unprofiled issue), against 1.86 in the morning's A/B without
the variant. Not measured: other prompts, longer generations, the variant
without COLI_CUDA_FLUSH, and the variant against the copy path without
QT_ASYNC_ISSUE (section 4's OFF arm runs ZC without the helper, but only
against ZC with it).
