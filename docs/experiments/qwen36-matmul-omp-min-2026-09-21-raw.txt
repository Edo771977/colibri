COLI_MATMUL_OMP_MIN: skip the OpenMP team for a matmul too small to pay for it
==============================================================================
RESULT: worth NOTHING on this host, and the arithmetic says why. Ships off
(default 0 = a team for every shape, i.e. no behaviour change). Kept with
its test as the B arm for a host whose OpenMP runtime is slower to open a
region.

Box      : Windows, RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 16 GB (sm_89), CUDA 13.4, CUDA_DLL=1
Engine   : qwen36_clang.exe (clang / LLVM libomp), rebuilt for this run
Model    : C:\modelli\qwen36_i4_gs64, prompt25.txt (25 prompt tokens)
Script   : misure-ompmin.ps1 (2 arms x 6 alternated reps at N_NEW=128, heat
           frozen after warm-up, NO COLI_CUDA_PROFILE -- this is a clean A/B)
Placement: COLI_PLACE=experts=0,lmhead=0,dnproj=0,dnout=0,attnout=0
Threads  : OMP_NUM_THREADS=16, OMP_WAIT_POLICY=ACTIVE, OMP_PROC_BIND=close
Arms     : thresh = COLI_MATMUL_OMP_MIN unset at the then-default 262144
           team   = COLI_MATMUL_OMP_MIN=0, a team for every shape
           (no rebuild between arms: the knob is read at runtime)


1. WHAT IT WAS AIMED AT
-----------------------

  qwen36's two DeltaNet gate projections, in_proj_a and in_proj_b, are
  [32 x 2048]: 65k FMAs each, about 4 us on one core. matmul() opened an
  OpenMP team for each of them, twice per DeltaNet layer, 30 DeltaNet
  layers -- SIXTY regions a decode token.

  The per-site dense profile put the pair at 0.86 ms/token: proj measured
  8.0 and dnproj's own wall accounted for 7.14 (238.1 us x 30).

  Predicted before the run, in the commit message and the script: proj down
  ~0.6 ms/token, and step() down by the same, because this is CPU work on a
  path with no GPU waiting on it.


2. THE MEASUREMENT
------------------

  arm      step()   deltanet   dn-sub proj   attention
  thresh    28.45     11.03        5.85         5.94
  team      28.60     11.06        5.85         5.86

  paired deltas (thresh - team), 6 pairs, negative = the threshold wins:

    step()        -0.15 ms/token   [-0.20 0.60 -0.80 -0.10 -0.20 0.70]  signs disagree
    deltanet      +0.07 ms/token   [-0.26 0.23 -0.43 0.03 0.11 0.26]    signs disagree
    dn-sub proj   +0.05 ms/token   [-0.20 0.10 -0.20 0.10 0.00 0.10]    signs disagree

  Identical generation in both arms (hit 100 %, miss(CPU) 0), as it must be:
  the change is bitwise identical by construction.

  The control behaved: `attention` holds no matmul under any sensible
  threshold and did not move (5.94 vs 5.86).

  Per-arm spread: thresh 1.80, team 0.50. The effect, if any, is far inside
  the noise of a single arm.


3. WHY THE PREDICTION WAS WRONG
-------------------------------

  Two mistakes, and the second is the interesting one.

  (a) The 0.86 ms/token came from a PROFILED pass, as a difference between
      two numbers that were both inflated by four cudaEventRecord per call.
      Unprofiled, proj is 5.85, not 8.0. A difference of inflated numbers is
      not an inflated difference, and treating it as one is how this got
      predicted at 0.6.

  (b) The 53 us region this was aimed at belongs to MinGW libgomp. Under
      LLVM libomp with OMP_WAIT_POLICY=ACTIVE the threads are already
      spinning and a region costs single-digit us:

          team    ~3 us region + 65k FMA / 16 threads  ~= 3.3 us
          serial   65k FMA on one core                 ~= 4.3 us

      The DeltaNet gate shape sits ON the break-even point. There was never
      anything to win here.

      Worse, a threshold calibrated for a 53 us region is far too high for a
      3 us one. At 262144 FMAs one core needs ~17 us and sixteen need ~1 + 3,
      so the shipped setting would have switched the team OFF where it still
      pays. The right value belongs to an OpenMP runtime, not to a shape --
      which is precisely why there is no good default.


4. WHAT SHIPPED
---------------

  COLI_MATMUL_OMP_MIN, default 0: a team for every shape, exactly the
  behaviour before the change. The knob, the test and this record stay.

  It is worth trying on a host where a region really does cost 53 us. This
  project measured 57.70 ms/token under MinGW libgomp against 32.73 under
  LLVM libomp on the same commit and container
  (qwen36-toolchain-2x-2026-09-19-raw.txt), so such hosts exist; there 60
  regions a decode token is ~3 ms. That is a hypothesis about another
  runtime, and hypotheses are not defaults.

  tests/test_matmul_omp_threshold.c asserts the two arms are bitwise
  identical on six shapes against a double reference, that the default is
  off, and that an explicitly set threshold lands between the gate shape and
  an ordinary trunk matmul.


5. WHAT THIS COSTS THE PLAN IT CAME FROM
----------------------------------------

  This change existed to replace a worse one: fusing in_proj_a and in_proj_b
  into the dnproj GEMV, which would have quantised them from f32 to int8
  per-row on the path computing beta and g -- the gates that multiply a
  persistent recurrent state every token. dn_qkv, dn_z and dn_out go through
  qdw_take; dn_a and dn_b deliberately do not.

  That fusion is still not worth making, and now for a second reason: the
  pair is not costing 0.86 ms/token, it is costing whatever is left after
  the profiler's own overhead is removed -- and the A/B above bounds the
  whole a/b OpenMP question at under 0.2 ms/token. There is no 0.86 to
  recover, with or without a numerics change.

  The dense round-trip finding from the same profile stands and is
  unaffected: 60 of DeltaNet's calls cost ~66-69 us of transfer each, with
  only ~11 us of launch/sync residue. That is where the time is.
