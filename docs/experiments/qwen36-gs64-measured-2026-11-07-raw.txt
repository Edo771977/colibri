Reading: the kernel change does what it was meant to on the GPU, and the
token does not see it. The shape is the one COLI_CUDA_DOWN_ROWS showed
alone in September (docs/ENVIRONMENT.md, that row): faster expert kernels,
the freed time reappearing in the dense GPU work that follows. Why is not
measured. Candidates: the GPU idles longer between the end of the expert
group and the next dense call and comes back slower (clocks, power state),
or the per-arm shift in dn/head is noise of an unpaired comparison. A
traced pair (Nsight Systems, the variable off and on) would show whether
the dense kernels themselves got longer. Not measured: other prompts,
longer generations, the variant without the zero-copy/async setting.
