lives in coli_cuda.dll, rebuilt ONLY by `make cuda-dll`. Three earlier kernel
measurements (PR #28's warp GEMV, and two sweeps of this kernel) rebuilt the
engine and not the DLL, so all four arms ran the same untouched DLL and came
back flat. Those results are void and are not reproduced here. Both sessions
below ran after `make cuda-dll CUDA_ARCH=sm_89`, with the script's staleness
guard confirming the DLL is newer than backend_cuda.cu.

READING
-------
Reproduces:
  - R>=2 beats R=0 by ~2x on kernel GB/s (187-191 -> 299-381) and by ~-13 %
    on step() (27.25-27.40 -> 23.4-23.9 ms/token, i.e. 36.6 -> 42.5 tok/s).
    No overlap between the R=0 arm and any other arm in either session.
  - R=8 is the slowest of the three R>=2 arms in both sessions, on both
    metrics.
  - measured/predicted falls off with R in the same shape both times
    (1.17-1.22 at R=2, 0.47-0.51 at R=8): the (1 + 4/R)x traffic model is not
    what governs this kernel past R=2.

Does NOT reproduce:
  - the ordering of R=2 vs R=4. R=4 won session 1 on both metrics, R=2 won
    session 2 on both. Their spreads overlap; the data does not separate them.
    Default picked as R=2 for lower register and shared-memory cost.

Not separable from this data:
  - row blocking (fewer x re-reads) vs the R*U independent-load ILP. Both
    landed in the same kernel; no arm isolates one of them.

Single GPU, single model, single shape mix. Nothing here says what this
kernel does on another architecture.
