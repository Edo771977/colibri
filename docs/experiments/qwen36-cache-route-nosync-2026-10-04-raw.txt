--- reading -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Determinism without the sync: docs/qwen36.md says two runs under the
    lever CAN differ without QT_UPLOAD_SYNC=1, because the asynchronous
    uploader decides what is resident when the experts are chosen. Here
    they did not: six ON runs, one text, one set of route meters, all
    equal to the sync record's. Of the counters printed per run (text,
    route meters, misses) only the misses moved, by one, in one run of each
    arm (OFF r4 6464, ON r1 3204). Why little can move, from the code
    (c/qwen36_tier.c): after warmstart, with the VRAM budget full (taken
    here from the residency, 6812/10240 in every run: a free budget would
    let decode queue plain uploads too), the only uploads in decode are the
    LFRU swaps, checked every 16 tokens at layer 0; the tick marks the
    victim non-resident at once in both modes, and only the newcomer's
    arrival is asynchronous (the uploader also waits for the in-flight
    group to be taken). So the two modes can differ only while a
    newcomer is in flight, which the one-miss differences fit. That is one
    prompt, 128 tokens and a mean of 8.0 LFRU swaps per run; a longer run,
    more swaps, or PILOT=1 can still diverge, so the waiver and the docs'
    caution stay.
  - The same decode sequences in both modes: with the same texts and the
    same route meters, this A/B and the sync one time the same token
    sequences; the two records differ in the sync itself, the session and
    the build, and these cannot be separated here. OFF 17.30 against
    17.42, ON 15.55 against 15.85; the 3 October OFF arm without sync
    (build 7549736, another session) read 17.43. The sync's own cost is
    still not measured, and these numbers do not suggest a large one.
  - Speed, as in the earlier records: the gain is cpu-miss (3.81 -> 1.94,
    ms per decode token) and the misses over the run (6463 -> 3203,
    prompt and decode rows); moe falls by 1.68, step() by 1.75; the
    other timers move by 0.08 or less, wait and take excepted (0.41 ->
    0.60 and 0.51 -> 0.69, wait being part of take), not attributed.
  - Position: the ON-first pairs read 0.43 ms/token more negative than the
    OFF-first ones (the sync record: -0.07). With six even pairs a
    constant position effect cancels in the mean. Rip 5 (ON 16.30, the
    slowest ON run, delta -1.20) carries about half of the difference:
    with a delta of -1.80 there it would be 0.23.
