CONSIST compares arm A (the whole prompt in one step(), S = 25) with
arms that prefill a prefix and decode the rest one token at a time
(split points 24, 19, 12, 2: 1 + 6 + 13 + 23 = 43 decode steps); with =1
those decode steps are the new call on all 30 layers. It stands in for
the plan's tiny-model acceptance, which needs torch on the operator's
machine to build the fixture. What it cannot see: anything both arms
share (its own comment in c/qwen36.c). The S > 1 prefill is not purely
the CPU: it still runs the placed dnproj GEMV on the GPU for every row
(qt_dnproj_matmul), and the new handle borrows that same device tensor,
so a defect in the placed dnproj weights is shared and invisible here.
What CONSIST does cover at =1 is the new call's own work -- the conv,
the recurrence, the gated norm and dnout on the GPU (the prefill does
out_proj through the CPU path) -- and the state upload. The gap with =1
is of the order of float rounding, four orders below the tolerance. No
other lines were read from the two logs.

--- reading --------------------------------------------------------------------

  - dn: 7.55 -> 3.75 ms/token (-3.80). The OFF arm's dn is the
    today's-path cost the plan started from (7.83 in section 10 of the
    27 September record). On the ON arm the whole call sits in the proj
    slot by design (plan stage 3, "dn-split/dn-gpu keep meaning
    something"), so dn-sub/dn-split show 0 in the other slots; dnproj
    and dnout count 3810/3810 in both arms (30 layers x 127 decode
    tokens). In the ON arm those two counters count the same single call
    (c/qwen36.c increments both), not two GEMV calls.
  - 3.75 ms/token over 30 layers is 125 us a layer, against 120.4 us
    for one call in the stage 2 bench (docs/qwen36-deltanet-gpu-plan.md,
    stage 2 "First run"): the bench's number holds in the engine to
    within ~5 us a layer.
  - The plan estimated 3.7-4 ms/token. dn alone gives -3.80; step() fell
    by 4.53. The other 0.73: moe -0.62, head -0.01, attn unchanged, and
    about -0.10 in the part of step() outside the four phases (22.25 -
    21.17 = 1.08 OFF, 17.72 - 16.74 = 0.98 ON). Inside moe the sub-timers
    moved as shared 3.44 -> 3.07, cpu-miss 4.15 -> 3.83, sh-ovl 3.45 ->
    3.08, wait +0.07, take +0.07; they overlap (shared and sh-ovl are
    nearly the same quantity), so they are not a breakdown of the -0.62.
    Why the MoE part is faster with the DeltaNet off the CPU is NOT
    measured. Candidates, none tested:
    the CPU team does no DeltaNet work between the MoE sections of
    consecutive layers (cache, memory bandwidth), or fewer hand-offs of
    the OpenMP team per token.
  - The miss count reads 6463 in all six OFF runs and 6463 or 6464 in
    the ON runs (the script prints the distinct values). Residency and
    hit rate are identical; one miss in ~6460 is not a factor here, but
    why at least one ON run differs by one is not known (the distinct
    values do not say how many ON runs read 6464).
  - Position: -0.40 between the two orders, inside the spread of the
    deltas (sd 0.49). Rep 4 (ON first) is the smallest delta: the
    fastest OFF run (21.60) paired with the slowest ON run (18.00).
  - In throughput terms (step() only, not the end-to-end wall): 44.9 ->
    56.4 tokens/s.

--- what this run did NOT measure ----------------------------------------------

  - Clocks, and whether the keep-alive actually ran (see section 2).
  - Longer generations (N_NEW 128 only), other prompts, other caps.
  - PPL=1: the real model has no reference file here. CONSIST (section
    1) is the correctness check that ran.
  - SERVE=1: COLI_DN_GPU is ignored there until stage 3b.
  - The end-to-end wall, prefill, and the time to first token.
  - The engine's failure recovery on the card: it is tested on the fake
    backend only (c/tests/test_qwen36_dn_gpu.c: a failed decode and a
    failed upload; a failed download has no fake switch and is not
    tested at all). The backend's own fault hook ran on the card in
    stage 2's test.
  - The state download. CONSIST resets with reset_recurrent, which marks
    the device copy stale without downloading, and a plain generate has
    no pin snapshot, so only the upload and the decode ran on the card.
  - A replication in another session or after a reboot.
  - The VRAM cost as such. Residency here is 6812/10240 against 6859
    in sections 9-10 of the 27 September record, 47 experts, which fits
    30 x 2.83 MiB of dnstate at ~1.8 MiB an expert; but that compares
    different runs and binaries, not an A/B.
  - Where the remaining ~125 us a layer goes (kernels, launches, the
    round trip). For scale, the stage 1 bench (`make deltanet-cuda-bench`)
    that the operator pasted with the stage 2 run, earlier on 28
    September and not committed anywhere else, read on this card:
    "back to back: 24.91 us per layer" for dn_conv_silu + dn_head,
    dn_conv_silu 5.67, dn_head 20.79, "empty kernel 5.50 us per layer"
    (the launch rate).
