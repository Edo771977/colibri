| qwen35 27B Q8_0 | 26.62 GiB | 26.90 B | CUDA | 99 | pp512 | 89.49 ± 0.14 |

1.78 tok/s does not mean llama.cpp is 12x slower than Colibri. That is a 27 B
DENSE model: it reads all 26.62 GiB of weights per token, against roughly 3 B
active parameters for a 35B-A3B. Nine times the traffic, and 26.6 GiB does not
fit in 16 GiB of VRAM, so the remainder crosses PCIe every token:

  ~16 GB in VRAM at ~670 GB/s     ->  ~24 ms
  ~12 GB in RAM over PCIe 4.0 x16 -> ~375 ms   (32 GB/s)
                                      ~400 ms  ->  ~2.5 tok/s
  measured 562 ms -> 1.78 tok/s

Same order of magnitude. The number was the model class, not the engine.
