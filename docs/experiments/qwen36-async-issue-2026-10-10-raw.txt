Reading: on this prompt the helper thread buys nothing by itself, and the
run shows why: the decode thread finishes its CPU work (cpu-miss + shared,
~4 ms/token) and then waits ~2-2.7 ms/token for the GPU. The kernels start
only at about the end of the launch call (6.8 us after it, median), while
the copies that open the graph (704 bytes and 8 KiB) start in the middle
of it: about 60 us of the 136 us group are before the first kernel. Why
the call is longer on the helper (52.8 against 33.5 us) is not measured;
the helper sharing a core with the OpenMP team is one candidate. Not
measured: the two switches separately under Nsight, other prompts.

What this leaves: with the helper on, the decode thread waits ~2 ms/token
(unprofiled) for the helper's launch plus the GPU group, so time removed
from that would count, up to about that wait and layer by layer. The next step (COLI_CUDA_GROUP_ZC, a graph of
kernels only) tests whether the copy-engine nodes are what makes the
launch long; that is a hypothesis.
