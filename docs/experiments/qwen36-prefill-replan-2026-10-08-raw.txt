--- reading -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The gain is the lever's target. cpu-miss falls by 1.45 ms per decode
    token (2.01 -> 0.56), and the misses over the whole run (prompt and
    decode rows) by 2070. moe falls by 1.26 and step() by 1.00. take
    rises 0.73 -> 0.87, and wait, which is part of take, 0.64 -> 0.78:
    more experts run on the GPU, and the CPU side finishes sooner. The
    meters do not separate the two, and the rise is not attributed.
  - The hit rates: 97.7 % covers the whole run, including the 25 prefill
    rows routed before the re-plan; 98.2 % covers the decode only. The
    OFF arm prints no decode-only line (it is printed under the re-plan
    only).
  - Fewer substitutions (4.5 % against 6.4 % on 4 October): with more of
    each token's own top experts resident, CACHE_ROUTE has fewer slots
    to substitute. Whether that brings the text closer to the plain
    router's is not shown: at position 121 the two agree, and the hashes
    differ. No perplexity was taken, so the quality is not measured.
  - The swaps cost the prefill: 147 swaps, each a cudaFree and a
    cudaMalloc per tensor in the uploader thread, plus the staging copies
    under the tier's lock. The script does not time the prefill or the
    TTFT, so that cost is not in these numbers.
  - The OFF arm reads 15.87 ms/token; on 4 October the same configuration
    read 15.55 (another session and build).
