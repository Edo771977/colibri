zero-copy group, at the edge of the noise: -0.52 ms/token over 10 pairs,
with an upper end of -0.02. The trace bounds what the mechanism can save.
The variant changes nothing before the first kernel, so the span it acts on
is from the end of the launch call to the results: (14.7 - 4.3) us per
group x 40 layers = ~0.4 ms/token (medians; 0.26 from the traces' per-token
sums). Counted from the start of the call, as the COLI_CUDA_GROUP_ZC record
does, it is ~0.55, which includes a launch call 32.7 -> 29.5 us shorter in
moe4 that the variant does not explain (one trace each, not paired). With
the launch on the helper the saving reaches the decode thread only through
wait, which fell 0.37 in the per-arm means. The 6-pair estimate (-1.00) is
